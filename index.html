Dinner, handled.

Potsy handles everything between "what's for dinner?" and the dishes.

🛡️

Tell Potsy once

Tell him about every allergy at your table, and he keeps those foods out of your recipes, your plan and your feed.

📅

Your week in one tap

Pick the days and Potsy plans 7 or 14 dinners that fit your budget. Lunches, breakfasts and desserts too, if you want them.

🛒

Your grocery list, done

Potsy writes the list for your whole week, sorted by store aisle, minus what's already in your pantry. Share it so everyone at home can add and tick things off.

🎁

Cooking for someone else

If a dish has something your table can't eat, Potsy says so clearly before it goes on your week, so nobody gets a surprise.

🍱

Meal prep

Cook once and eat well for days. Pick how many days and which meals, and Potsy picks recipes everyone at your table can eat.

📷

Scan it, know how long it keeps

Scan a barcode and Potsy tells you what it is, how long it lasts in the pantry or fridge, and which recipes use it.

⏱️

Steps anyone can follow

Every recipe is written one clear step at a time, with timers built in and the step read aloud, so you're never scrolling with messy hands.

🦃

Groups and potlucks

Plan Thanksgiving or a birthday. Potsy starts the menu, everyone joins with a code and picks what they're bringing. Made with love. Meant to be shared.

🎃

Seasons and holidays

Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas and more: seasonal favorites on your home screen, and Potsy dresses for the occasion.

🧺

Farmers markets nearby

Find the markets around you on a real map, with days, hours and directions.

📸

Share what you cooked

Post tonight's dinner or a short cooking video, follow other home cooks, and save the dishes you want to make.

🎉

Potsy cheers you on

Finish a recipe or level up and Potsy celebrates with you. Climb from Line Cook to Pot Legend and unlock new looks for him.

Meet Potsy, your kitchen buddy.

Potsy is your chef friend in the app. Ask him for dinner ideas, what to make with what's in your fridge, or how anything works. He knows your table, so his answers fit your family, allergies and all.

And he's yours: paint him any color you like and dress him from a big free wardrobe of hats, eyes, aprons and little buddies. He even dresses up for the holidays.
