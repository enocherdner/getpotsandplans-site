Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-20.

The short version

Pots & Plans helps you plan meals and share food with people you choose. We keep only what the app needs to do that, we never sell it, and you can delete your account and everything in it from Settings at any time.

What we keep

Your account: your name, email address and password (stored scrambled by our provider, never readable by us), or your Apple or Google sign-in.

Your kitchen: your answers (household size, budget, diet, allergies and foods you skip), your household people, plans, grocery lists, pantry, saved recipes and ratings. Allergies count as health information. We keep them private and use them only to keep recipes and posts safe for your table. Other people never see whose allergy it is.

What you share: photos, videos, captions, comments, chat messages, groups and the recipes you post.

Your phone: a notification token if you allow notifications, so we can tell you about likes, comments and messages.

What we never keep

Your exact location. The farmers market map uses it on your phone only and saves just the ZIP code you type. We do not track you across other apps or websites, show ads, or use analytics that follow you.

Who can see what

Your posts, profile, comments and likes can be seen by other Pots & Plans cooks. Your chats can be seen by the people in them. Your answers, allergies, household, plans, pantry and lists are private, except that the people in your household share one grocery list and see the allergies at its table, without names.

New photos and videos may get a quick automatic check for nudity and violence before others can see them, and people can report anything. Moderators can see reported things to decide what to do.

Who helps us run it

Supabase stores the app’s data and files in the United States and runs our sign-in. Expo passes notifications to Apple and Google, who deliver them to your phone. Apple and Google also handle sign-in if you use their accounts. If the automatic image check is on, OpenAI checks new pictures and does not keep them for training. Each of them may use your data only to do that job for us, never for anything of their own.

Potsy answers from the app itself today, so nothing you ask Potsy leaves your phone. If we turn on Potsy’s smarter AI answers later, the questions you ask Potsy would be sent to Anthropic to answer them, and we will update this policy before that happens.

Children

Pots & Plans is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep their data. If you think a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Deleting your account

Settings, then Delete my account, removes your account, posts, photos, videos, comments, messages, plans, lists and answers. Groups you started pass to the next person who joined. Copies in our backups are gone within 30 days.

Questions

Write to us at the support email on our App Store or Google Play page.

If we change this policy we will say so in the app before the change applies.
