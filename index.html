Dinner, handled.

Everything between "what's for dinner?" and the dishes, in one app.

📅

Your week in one tap

Pick the days, and get 7 or 14 dinners that fit your budget. Lunches, breakfasts and desserts too, if you want them.

🛡️

Allergy safe

Tell it once. Recipes and posts with an allergen anyone at your table avoids never show up.

🛒

The list writes itself

Every ingredient lands on one list, sorted by store aisle, minus what's already in your pantry.

👨‍👩‍👧

Shared with your household

One grocery list on every phone at home. Anyone can add things or tick them off at the store.

🦃

Groups and potlucks

Start a group for the family or for Thanksgiving, split the menu, and see who's bringing what.

🧺

Farmers markets nearby

A map of the markets around you, with days, hours and directions.

📸

Share what you cooked

Post tonight's dinner or a short cooking video, follow other home cooks, and save what you want to make.

⏱️

Cook mode

One big step at a time with built-in timers, so you're never scrolling with messy hands.

🏅

Level up

Climb from Line Cook to Pot Legend as you cook and share.
